About Our Institution

Ariyava Montessori Matriculation Higher Secondary School, Madappuram, is a premier educational institution dedicated to holistic development. Rooted in the timeless Montessori philosophy, we blend child-centered learning with the rigour of the Tamil Nadu Matriculation Board.

With over 20 years of trust, we have shaped more than a generation of students into confident, compassionate, and capable individuals ready to face the world.

Student Behaviour & Conduct

  • Students are expected to be polite, respectful, and obedient to teachers and staff. Bullying, fighting, using foul language, or shouting is strictly prohibited.
  • Regular attendance is mandatory, often with a minimum percentage (e.g., 80% or higher) required. Late arrivals are often penalized with fines or detention.
  • Students must wear the prescribed, clean, and ironed uniform. Strict grooming standards, such as short hair for boys and neatly tied hair for girls, are enforced.

Campus & Academic Policies

  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, and other electronic gadgets are generally banned or must be switched off, and are often confiscated if found.
  • Cheating, plagiarism, or malpractice in tests and assignments is forbidden.
  • Students must not damage school property, such as furniture or walls, and must keep the campus clean.
  • Bringing valuable items, fancy stationery, jewellery, or sharp objects to school is forbidden.

DR.M.KESAVAPANDIAN,

SECRETARY

At Ariyava, we believe that true education goes beyond textbooks; it is about building character and supporting every child's journey, regardless of the hurdles they face.

As the Secretary, I am proud to oversee an institution that stands on three pillars: Academic Brilliance, Athletic Excellence, and Social Responsibility. We are deeply committed to providing world-class education and professional sports training to all our students.

However, our heart lies in our mission to support children battling illness. We strive to ensure that health challenges never stand in the way of a child's potential. By fostering an inclusive environment of care and high standards, we prepare our students to not only succeed in their careers but to lead with kindness.
